About the text

The Hanuman Chalisa is in Awadhi, a form of Hindi. The Ram Raksha Stotram and Kal Bhairav Ashtakam are in Sanskrit, written in the same Devanagari script.

Verses were compared across Sanskrit Wikisource, Shlokam.org, Vachmi and HinduTone. Recitation traditions differ slightly, so your family's version may vary by a word or two. English meanings were written for this site and are a plain guide, not a scholarly translation.

The English view spells the same words in English letters, without the marks scholars use for exact pronunciation, so treat it as a reading aid.

Recordings are hosted on YouTube and belong to their channels. YouTube loads only after you press Listen.

Sing along uses your browser's speech recognition, which may send audio to your browser maker's servers while it is on. This site stores no audio.
